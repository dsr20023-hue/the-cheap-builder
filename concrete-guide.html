Concrete ordering: a simple estimate

For a rectangular slab, length × width × depth gives cubic feet; divide by 27 for cubic yards.

Example

A 30 × 40 ft slab at 5 inches is about 18.52 cubic yards raw, or about 20.37 cubic yards with 10% waste.

Why the final order can differ

  • Uneven subgrade or forms.
  • Footings, thickened edges or pads.
  • Truck access and supplier requirements.

Confirm final dimensions and mix requirements with your plans and concrete supplier.

This is an estimating aid, not engineering advice.
