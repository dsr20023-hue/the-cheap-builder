PACKOUT: what to compare before you buy

Buy around your workflow instead of collecting boxes.

1. Daily access

  • Use drawers for tools you reach for constantly.
  • Use large boxes for bulky equipment.
  • Use organizers for small parts, bits and fasteners.

2. Total system cost

Price the complete setup you need, including drawers, organizers and accessories.

3. Used storage

Check slides, latches, wheels, handles and cracks. A dirty box can be a bargain; broken hardware can erase the savings.

Before buying: Write your exact shopping list, price it new, then compare used listings and current promotions.
